# Hearts

Standard Hearts. **Goal: avoid penalty points.** Lower score is better.

## Setup & Passing

- Deal 13 cards to each player.
- Passing rotates by round:
  - **Round 1:** pass 3 cards to the **left**
  - **Round 2:** pass 3 cards to the **right**
  - **Round 3:** pass 3 cards **across**
  - **Round 4:** **no passing**
- **Any three cards may be passed**, penalty cards included — the Q♠ and hearts are fair game.

## Play

- The holder of the **2♣ leads** the first trick with it.
- Standard trick-taking, no trump: follow suit if able; highest card of the led suit wins; winner leads next.
- **No penalty cards** (hearts or Q♠) may be played on the first trick unless a player has no other option.
- **Hearts may not be led** until hearts have been broken (a heart played on an earlier trick). Exception: a player holding only hearts may lead one.

## Scoring

- Each **heart** taken in tricks: **+1**
- **Q♠:** **+13**
- **Shooting the moon** (capturing all hearts and the Q♠): shooter scores **0**, every other player scores **+26**.

## Markers

- **□** lowest score this round · **▼** highest score this round
- **Moon shot:** the shooter gets □, all other players get ▼.
