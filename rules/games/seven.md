# Seven

A Fan Tan / Sevens variant. **Goal: discard as few points as possible.** Lower score is better.

## Setup

- Deal the entire deck: 13 cards to each player.
- The **starting suit** rotates by round: Round 1 = ♣, Round 2 = ♦, Round 3 = ♥, Round 4 = ♠.

## Play

- The holder of the starting suit's 7 (e.g., 7♣ in round 1) goes first and **must play it**.
- The other suits' 7s may be played at any time to open their lines.
- On your turn, play a card adjacent to an existing card of the same suit (building up toward the King / down toward the 2 from each 7).
- **You must play if you have a legal play.** If you have several, choose freely.
- If you have no legal play, you **must discard** one card face down. Discards are hidden from others, but you may review your own.
- The game runs exactly 13 turns per player — every card ends up played or discarded.

## Ace Convention

- The **first ace placed** in a round decides, for **all suits** that round, whether aces go **under the 2** or **above the King**.
- Terminal cards follow from this: aces above kings → terminals are 2 (bottom) and A (top); aces under 2s → terminals are A (bottom) and K (top).

## Line Collapse

- When a suit's line reaches a **terminal card at either end, the entire line dies** — no further cards may be played on that suit in either direction.
- Cards of a collapsed suit become forced discards.

## Scoring

Sum the values of your **discarded** cards:

- **2–10:** face value
- **J:** 11 · **Q:** 12 · **K:** 13
- **A:** 1 if aces went under 2s this round, 14 if above kings, **7 if no ace was placed at all**

## Markers

- **□** lowest score this round · **▼** highest score this round
